2026-11-14  kodak_portra_endura.json, kodak_2383.json: made from spektrafilm's print profiles
            kodak_portra_endura.json and kodak_2383.json at its commit 3bb2c2d. The curves, dye spectra and base
            are resampled to 1 nm with gaps held at the nearest measured value; the observer under the viewing
            light, the exposure of an 18 % grey and the matrices from RP 180 and Academy Printing Density are
            computed from them and added. Modified by Alpha Lam.
